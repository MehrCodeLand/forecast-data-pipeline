About This Project

Who We Are

This project is an independent weather data pipeline. We collect current conditions for each tracked city on a fixed schedule and show the results to you.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make raw weather history accessible and understandable.

The Data We Collect

For each city we store temperature, wind speed, wind direction, day/night flag and weather code, together with a timestamp.
